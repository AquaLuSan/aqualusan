Our story

From Taiwan to Australia

AquaLuSan began with two founders who studied Aquaculture at National Taiwan Ocean University. A shared passion for ornamental fish and shrimp led us to Australia, where many people love aquatic life, but quality tanks and care are not always easy to access.

We bring knowledge and products from Taiwan’s aquarium industry to Australia, so homes, restaurants, cafés, offices and hotels can enjoy a well-designed aquarium that is professionally maintained.
