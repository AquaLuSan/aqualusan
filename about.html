Our story

From Taiwan to Australia

AquaLuSan began with two Taiwanese founders who both studied Aquaculture at university in Taiwan.

Our shared passion for ornamental fish and shrimp led us to talk about the differences between the aquarium cultures in Taiwan and Australia. We soon realised that many Australians have a genuine love for ornamental fish and aquatic life, but the aquarium hobby is not yet as accessible or widely established here as it is in Taiwan.

That became the starting point for AquaLuSan.

We wanted to bring our knowledge, experience and connections from Taiwan’s well-established aquarium industry to Australia — making quality aquarium products and professional services more accessible to people who share our passion.

What we do

Today, AquaLuSan focuses on two key areas.

Aquarium equipment import & wholesale

We import quality aquarium products from established Taiwanese brands, connecting Taiwan’s aquarium industry with the Australian market.

我們的故事

AquaLuSan

AquaLuSan，始於兩個來自台灣、同樣就讀海洋大學水產養殖學系的人。

因為喜歡觀賞魚蝦，我們開始聊起台灣與澳洲的水族文化。慢慢地，我們發現，澳洲其實有許多人喜愛觀賞魚蝦，只是相較於台灣，觀賞水族在澳洲的市場與文化使得一般民眾不敢輕易嘗試或是嘗試過後擁有不好的經驗。

這也成為我們創立 AquaLuSan 的起點。

我們希望把台灣多年累積的水族專業、產品與經驗帶到澳洲，讓更多人可以更輕鬆、更親近地享受觀賞水族的樂趣。

AquaLuSan 目前專注於兩大領域

Aquarium Equipment Import & Wholesale
引進台灣優質水族品牌與產品，連結台灣與澳洲水族市場。

Aquarium Design & Maintenance
提供專業魚缸設計、造景、安裝、水質管理，以及活體與水草日常維護服務。

我們相信，一座好的水族缸不只是魚缸，而是一個能為空間帶來生命、美感與放鬆感的水中世界。

無論是家中、餐廳、咖啡廳、辦公室、飯店或商業空間，我們希望用專業與熱情，為您打造一個賞心悅目的水族景觀。
